The "Float Test" for Egg Freshness

Before throwing away eggs past their carton date, place one gently in a bowl of cold water. If it sinks to the bottom and lays flat, it's very fresh. If it stands on its end, it's still safe to eat (cook thoroughly). If it floats to the top, toss it!
